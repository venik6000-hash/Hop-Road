Changes in this project: reconstructed materials from packed images, normalized
scale, baked skeletal animations, added work and service animations, exported GLB.
The modified monk model and its animations are distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
Original source files are included under source/; source/.gdignore keeps them
out of Godot's automatic Blender importer.

Innkeeper and Maid — generated by the project's owner with Tencent Hunyuan 3D
(https://3d.hunyuan.tencent.com/creation). Thinned, scaled, given a skeleton and weights
(tools/pipeline/npc/sculpt.py); the maid's irises were moved to look ahead.

Recorded motion (assets/models/npc/motion) — the data was obtained from
mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
BVH conversion: https://github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap. Parts of the
recordings were cut, looped and carried onto the villagers' skeletons
(tools/pipeline/npc/motion.py, motion_clips.json).
