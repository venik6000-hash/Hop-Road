BAR PROPS (keg.glb, rag.glb, jug.glb, stream.glb)
Built by the scripts in tools/pipeline/bar/ (Blender). Sources:

keg.glb
  Barrel: "Wine Barrel 01" by James Ray Cock, Poly Haven, CC0 1.0
  https://polyhaven.com/a/wine_barrel_01 (mesh and 2K maps)
  Changes: scaled to 0.40 x 0.30 m, laid on its side, the four parts joined.
  Cradle: modelled for this project; texture "Rough Wood", Poly Haven, CC0 1.0
  https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_wood (tinted to dark oak, 1K).
  Brass tap and lever: modelled for this project, tarnish baked procedurally.

rag.glb
  Folded cloth modelled for this project; texture "Rough Linen", Poly Haven,
  CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen (bleached to raw linen,
  stained, resampled onto the sheet).

jug.glb
  "Jug 01", Poly Haven, CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/a/jug_01
  Changes: scaled to 0.24 m, handle turned to +X; colour, roughness and
  normal maps re-baked as stoneware (the scan's normal map is kept inside
  the new one).

stream.glb
  Modelled for this project.
