RIGHT HAND
Free sample full-body scan by Ten24 (3D Scan Store)
https://ten24.info/sample-scan/
Terms on the page: "Feel free to use this scan as you wish for both commercial
and personal projects", with credit to Ten24.
Changes (assets/models/source/beer/scan_hand.glb): cut the right forearm and
hand from the subdivision level 3 OBJ; finger joints found from the mesh and a
skeleton with the old hand's bone names added, weights by Blender's automatic
weights; UVs packed for the arm alone; the 16k colour and the level 3 normal
map baked to 4096 px and scaled to 2048 px. prepare_hand.py then closes the
hand round the handle. The cut, rig and bake were run once and are not kept.

Linen sleeve: procedural geometry authored for this project.
Fluid: native Godot implementation of Position Based Fluids (Macklin & Müller,
2013), informed by dgreenheck/threejs-particle-fluids; no Three.js runtime or
source files are bundled. Foam is a procedural surface appearance on the liquid.
