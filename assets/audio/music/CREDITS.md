# Music

Three loops by RandomMind from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org), released under **Creative Commons 0** (public domain dedication: no attribution required; credited here anyway). The licence was read on each track's own page.

`tools/pipeline/music/prepare_audio.py` downloads the loop versions named in `SOURCES.json`, checks their SHA-256, brings each to -18 LUFS and writes it as Ogg Vorbis, 112 kbit/s. `MusicService` plays them.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `bards_tale.ogg` | main menu | [Medieval: The Bard's Tale](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-bards-tale) | RandomMind | CC0 1.0 |
| `old_tower_inn.ogg` | the valley | [Medieval: The Old Tower Inn](https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-the-old-tower-inn) | RandomMind | CC0 1.0 |
