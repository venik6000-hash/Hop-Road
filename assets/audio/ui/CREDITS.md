# Menu sounds

Four sounds from the pack [50 RPG sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl), released under **Creative Commons 0** (public domain dedication: no attribution required; credited here anyway). The licence was read on the pack's page and in its `license.txt`.

`tools/pipeline/music/prepare_audio.py` downloads the pack named in `SOURCES.json`, checks its SHA-256 and copies the files unchanged. `UiSounds` plays them.

| File | Use | Source file | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hover.ogg` | the pointer or the focus comes to a button | `handleSmallLeather.ogg` | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `click.ogg` | a button is pressed | `metalLatch.ogg` | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `open.ogg` | a menu or a panel opens | `bookOpen.ogg` | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
| `close.ogg` | a menu or a panel closes | `bookClose.ogg` | Kenney | CC0 1.0 |
